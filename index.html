About Ocean Guardians

We are a global organization dedicated to protecting marine ecosystems and all forms of ocean life.

Our Mission

Ocean Guardians works tirelessly to combat ocean pollution, restore coral reefs, protect endangered marine species, and promote sustainable fishing practices worldwide.

Founded in 2015, we now operate in over 80 countries with more than 120,000 volunteers and marine experts.

We combine scientific research, direct action, and policy advocacy to ensure healthy oceans for future generations.
